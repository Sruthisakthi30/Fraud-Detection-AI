## 3. In-Depth Comparative Observations

### 3.1 Supervised Classifiers
1. **XGBoost (Overall Best Model):**
   - Achieved the highest balanced performance across both Precision ($90.00\%$) and Recall ($90.00\%$), yielding an F1-score of **$0.9000$** and ROC-AUC of **$0.9996$**.
   - Second-order gradient boosting with tree shrinkage effectively captured non-linear boundary contours between high-dimensional PCA vectors ($V_4, V_14, V_1$).
2. **Logistic Regression (High Recall Baseline):**
   - Delivered exceptional Recall ($95.00\%$), successfully intercepting 19 out of 20 fraud attempts in the test set.
   - However, Precision was lower ($65.52\%$), generating 10 False Positives. This behavior stems from balanced class weighting aggressively expanding the decision boundary.
3. **Random Forest (High Precision Ensemble):**
   - Achieved strong Precision ($81.25\%$) but lower Recall ($65.00\%$), missing 7 fraudulent transactions.
   - Ensemble voting favored conservative classification in boundary overlap regions.
4. **Decision Tree (Baseline Tree):**
   - Exhibited expected susceptibility to local noise and synthetic SMOTE points, achieving F1 of $0.5957$ with 13 False Positives.

### 3.2 Unsupervised Anomaly Detection (Isolation Forest)
- Isolation Forest achieved an F1-score of $0.0741$.
- **Critical Theoretical Finding:** In contrast to supervised classifiers that learn explicit labels, Isolation Forest measures feature-space sparsity. Many legitimate transactions in real-world finance contain rare amounts or off-peak timestamps that make them statistical anomalies without being fraudulent.
- **Architectural Utility:** Isolation Forest is invaluable for detecting zero-day attack vectors unseen in historical training sets, but must be paired with human triage rather than automated blocking.

## 5. Financial Cost-Benefit & Business Trade-Off Analysis

In payment processing, errors carry asymmetric economic costs:
- **Cost of False Negative ($C_{\text{FN}}$):** Direct financial chargeback loss + scheme non-compliance fine $\approx \$150.00$ per undetected fraud.
- **Cost of False Positive ($C_{\text{FP}}$):** Customer support friction + interchange processing + potential churn $\approx \$15.00$ per declined cardholder.

### Total Expected Cost Equation:
$$\text{Cost} = (FN \times C_{\text{FN}}) + (FP \times C_{\text{FP}})$$

| Model | False Negatives | False Positives | Chargeback Loss ($) | Friction Cost ($) | Total Operational Cost ($) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Naive Baseline (Detect 0)** | 20 | 0 | $3,000.00 | $0.00 | **$3,000.00** |
| **XGBoost** | 2 | 2 | $300.00 | $30.00 | **$330.00** (89.0% Cost Reduction) |
| **Logistic Regression** | 1 | 10 | $150.00 | $150.00 | **$300.00** (90.0% Cost Reduction) |
| **Random Forest** | 7 | 3 | $1,050.00 | $45.00 | **$1,095.00** (63.5% Cost Reduction) |

### Key Business Takeaways:
1. **Model Selection Depends on Risk Appetite:** While XGBoost achieves superior balanced F1 and precision, Logistic Regression is optimal for high-risk merchants where missing even a single fraud is unacceptable ($C_{\text{FN}} \gg C_{\text{FP}}$).
2. **Threshold Tuning:** Lowering the decision threshold from $0.50$ to $0.35$ in XGBoost increases recall to $95\%$ while keeping false positives below $1.5\%$.
